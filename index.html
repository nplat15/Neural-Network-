05  BEHIND THE PREDICTION

A pattern learner,
not a crystal ball.

The neural network learns combinations of queue size, staffing, time of day, and weekday from 2,000 clinic observations. We held out 20% of the records to compare it with a simple baseline that always predicts the training-set average wait.

The dataset source is not documented in the provided files. These holdout results do not show how the model performs on a different clinic or dataset. The data shows patterns, not proof that staffing changes alone cause a specific change in wait time.
